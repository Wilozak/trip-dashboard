Check-in, checkout, early/late, and day-use explained
  • Normal overnight booking (what you want): a room from the check-in time on Oct 2 until the checkout time on Oct 5.
  • Check-in time is usually 12 PM–3 PM. If yours is 12 PM, arriving at 2–3 PM is fine; the room should be ready.
  • If check-in is 2 PM or 3 PM and you arrive earlier, you may have to wait or pay for early check-in. Ask when you call.
  • Checkout is usually 10 AM, 11 AM or 12 PM on Monday. If your return flight is late, ask about late checkout or leave bags at reception.
  • Day-use / hourly hotels (sold in 3–12 hour slots) are not what you're looking for. Don't book an ‘hourly’ rate by mistake on OYO, Brevistay or Bag2Bag.

Fri Oct 2 is Gandhi Jayanti, a fixed national public holiday, and it starts a 3-day weekend. Expect larger crowds at ISKCON and higher hotel demand than a normal Friday. ISKCON uses its weekend/public-holiday timings (no afternoon closure). Gandhi Jayanti is also typically a dry day in India (no alcohol sales).

    Policy & questions to ask

    Four different things that are easy to mix up.

    These are not the same

    Couple friendly
    A marketing tag. Usually means the hotel doesn't turn couples away, but it's not a contract.
    Unmarried couples accepted
    An explicit check-in policy. OYO lets partner hotels decline couples at their discretion (since Jan 2025), so even this can change at the desk.
    Local ID accepted
    Whether someone with a Bengaluru/Karnataka address on their ID can check in. Some hotels only accept non-local guests.
    Day visitors allowed
    Whether a friend who isn't registered can come to your room. No hotel on this list states its policy.

    What's typical for visitors (general, not hotel-specific)

    A travel-booking guide from ixigo says visitors are usually allowed roughly between 10 AM and 8 PM, must register at reception with a valid ID, and can be refused at the hotel's discretion. An overnight visitor usually has to be added as a guest, sometimes for a fee.

    That's a general pattern, not a policy of any hotel here. If she might stay the night, the cleanest option is to book for 2 guests and register both of you at check-in.

    Real refusals happen. Guests on Quora and review sites describe hotels refusing check-in even after an OYO booking, including one who had phoned ahead. Always carry original ID, and have a backup hotel in mind.

    Call / WhatsApp script

    1. We're two adults, unmarried. Will you check us in for Oct 2 to Oct 5, three nights?
    2. One of us has a Bengaluru/Karnataka address on our ID. Is local ID accepted?
    3. Which IDs do you accept? Aadhaar, driving licence, passport, voter ID? Is PAN accepted?
    4. Can a friend visit my room during the day without staying overnight? What hours? Does she need to show ID or register? Is there a fee?
    5. We'll arrive around 2–3 PM. Will the room be ready? Is there an early check-in charge?
    6. What time is checkout on Monday? Is late checkout or bag storage possible?
    7. If I book through OYO / MakeMyTrip / FabHotels, will you honour that booking and price at the desk?
    8. Is there reliable hot water and AC in the room? Is there a lift?
    9. Please confirm the couple and ID answers on WhatsApp.

        Friday: BLR → hotel

        Airport bus KIA-10
        Vayu Vajra (BMTC, AC Volvo). Stops include Hebbal, Mekhri Circle, Yeshwanthpur TTMC, ISKCON, Rajajinagar, then on to Vijayanagar and Mysuru Road. Full route ≈ 97–105 min.
        Bus fare
        ₹300–360 for KIA-10 (unofficial fare chart). Other unofficial sites give ₹150–400 across routes. Fares change, so check the Namma BMTC app for the current KIA-10 fare.
        Bus frequency
        KIA-10 roughly every 30–60 min (unofficial tracker).
        Airport bus KIA-9
        To Majestic via Palace Guttahalli and Mekhri Circle, ≈ 55–75 min. Useful for the Ramachandrapuram hotel near KSR.
        Cab
        Rajajinagar ≈ 34 km, ≈ 48 min in light traffic. Quoted fares: KSTDC ≈ ₹817 (day), Ola Prime Sedan ≈ ₹1,151 (TaxiAutoFare). App surge can push this higher.
        Where to get off
        ISKCON stop for Kingston/Bloom; Yeshwanthpur TTMC for Yeshwanthpur hotels; Rajajinagar for Thimmaiah Rd / Grand Regency side; KIA-9 to Majestic for Ramachandrapuram.

        My suggestion: take a cab if you want to be checked in by ~14:30 with checked luggage. Take KIA-10 if saving ₹500–800 matters more and arriving ~15:00–15:30 is fine. Both are estimates; Friday holiday traffic can change them.
